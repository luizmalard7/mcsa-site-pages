# Prompts das composições ilustrativas

Imagens criadas com o gerador de imagens integrado, usando os retratos da equipe publicados pela MCSA como referências. As cenas são composições, não fotografias de reuniões reais.

## Marcelo Candiotto e Carolina Sant’Ana

Arquivo: [`marcelo-carolina.png`](../assets/images/institucional/marcelo-carolina.png)

> Use case: photorealistic-natural image editing for the MCSA law-firm website hero. The first reference photo is Marcelo Candiotto; the second is Carolina Sant’Ana. Create a new wide horizontal editorial photograph showing exactly these two people together, standing side by side in a refined contemporary Brazilian office, naturally reviewing a dark folder and discussing a case. Preserve their recognizable facial identity, hair, approximate age and professional appearance from the reference portraits. Make Marcelo and Carolina equally prominent, visible from the waist up, with both faces unobstructed and generous crop room around them for a responsive website. Warm daylight, charcoal and cream clothing, warm wood and subtle brass details to suit the existing charcoal/cream/gold site palette. Realistic faces and hands, tasteful natural expression, photographic quality. No extra people, no logos, no readable text, no watermarks, no gavels, no scales of justice.

## Marcelo Candiotto e Rodrigo Valle

Arquivo: [`marcelo-rodrigo-valle.png`](../assets/images/institucional/marcelo-rodrigo-valle.png)

> Use case: photorealistic-natural image editing for the MCSA law-firm website About section. The first reference photo is Marcelo Candiotto; the second is Rodrigo Valle. Create a new wide horizontal editorial photograph showing exactly these two people together at a contemporary office meeting table, reviewing a case file and discussing it naturally. Preserve each person's recognizable facial identity, hair, beard, approximate age and professional appearance from the reference portraits; Marcelo should resemble the first reference and Rodrigo the second. Both faces must be clearly visible, with comfortable room for a 4:3 website crop. Warm daylight, charcoal suits, cream paper, warm wood and restrained brass details consistent with a charcoal/cream/gold site. Photographic realism, plausible hands and documents without readable text, collaborative and confident mood. No extra people, no logos, no watermarks, no courtroom props.
